      Supported formats
      • Chase — Transaction Date, Description, Category, Amount
      • American Express — Date, Description, Amount

      Settings

      Bank sync

      Connect a bank or card through Plaid and purchases show up on their own. Prism checks when it opens and every 15 minutes while running. Plaid covers US banks; anything else can still be imported from statements.

      Finish connecting in your browser — this page updates on its own when you're done. Didn't open? Open Plaid

      Get keys at dashboard.plaid.com → Developers → Keys. Each environment has its own secret. In Sandbox, pick any bank and sign in with user_good / pass_good. Stored only on your computer, never shared.

      Currency

      Dollars are what Prism calculates with. A purchase made in shekels keeps its ₪ amount and shows it next to the dollar figure — choose an ILS statement when importing, or shekels when adding a transaction.

      The daily rate is fetched when needed and cached, so past imports keep the rate of their day.

      Claude AI optional

      Prism categorizes, cleans up and analyzes on its own. An Anthropic API key adds Claude for the leftovers: merchants nothing recognizes, web-search identification, and written summaries.

      From console.anthropic.com → API Keys. Stored only on your computer.

      Clean up

      Merge duplicates
      Finds merchants that are really the same one — spelling variants, "Amazon Mktpl" vs "Amazon" — and lets you review before merging.
      Re-check categories
      Runs the latest rules over categories Prism guessed on its own. Anything you set yourself is left exactly as it is.
      Categorize the rest
      Applies your merchant memory and the built-in rules to anything still uncategorized. With an API key, Claude takes a pass at what's left.
      Tidy merchant names
      Strips transaction codes, store numbers, and payment-processor prefixes from names imported by older versions.
      Remove payments
      Deletes bill payments, autopay and transfers that slipped through an import.

      Data lives in your app folder with daily backups — see the About section below to open it.
